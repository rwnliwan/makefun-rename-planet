Entry for [Rename the Planet](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369) ($3.10 pot, 10 paid places, deadline 27 Nov 2026 00:05 WITA). One line, under 200 characters, giving a planet of our solar system a funny new name and saying why in the same breath.

## The entry

> Rename Uranus "The Barrel" — it rolls around the Sun on its side, tilted 98 degrees, like a barrel someone kicked.

114 characters. Uranus is the only planet knocked onto its side at a 98-degree axial tilt, so it rolls around the Sun instead of spinning upright. The name is a mundane Earth object; the reason leans on a trait no other planet has.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` and checks the 1–200 character gate before writing. Run with `node main.ts` (Node 22+).
